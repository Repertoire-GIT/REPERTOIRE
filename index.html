Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. The app works fully offline.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The developer can't see your workouts. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I back up my workouts, or move to a new phone?

Repertoire saves a backup on your phone after every workout, and these are part of your iPhone or iCloud backup. To keep a copy somewhere else, or to move to a phone without restoring an iPhone backup: Settings → Backup → Export all data, save the file (for example to iCloud Drive), then on the new phone open Repertoire and choose Import a backup.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored, including its automatic backups. Export a copy first if you might want it back.

How do the ranks work?

Your best lifts are compared with your body weight, and your best jumps, sprints, runs and rows with fixed standards, giving an Upper body, Lower body, Conditioning and Overall rank from Starter to Legend. It's the app's own ranking, not an official standard, and you can switch ranks off in Settings.

How do I get a training plan?

Train → Routines → Get a plan. Repertoire writes a prompt with your goal, schedule and equipment; paste it into any AI chat you use, then paste the answer back. The app itself never contacts an AI service.
